  Used for  The country shown next to a server, and the country
            filter

  Attribution: Twemoji, https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji,
  licensed CC BY 4.0.

  Each file carries the two-letter ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code; the
  upstream asset is the pair of Unicode regional-indicator letters
  for that code (de.png, for example, is 1f1e9-1f1ea.png).
  _unknown.png is Twemoji's globe glyph (1f310.png).

  Changes, stated as CC BY 4.0 section 3(a)(1)(B) requires: every
  asset was cropped to its non-transparent bounding box and centred
  on a transparent 96x72 canvas so that the images share one size
  in the server list. Nothing was recoloured or redrawn.

  Roxa claims no copyright and no licence over these files. They
  are NOT covered by the GPL-3.0 that covers Roxa's code, and
  nothing in this file should be read as relicensing them. The
  two-letter codes are ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 values, which are not
  subject to copyright; the licence above covers the artwork.
