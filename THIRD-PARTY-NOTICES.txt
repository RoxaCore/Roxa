6. Country flag images
================================================================

  Files     Assets\Flags\*.png  (90 files: 89 two-letter country
            codes plus _unknown.png, the fallback used when no
            code matches)
  License   Not recorded - see below
  Source    Not recorded
  Used for  The country shown next to a server, and the country
            filter

  These images entered this repository in a single commit with no
  accompanying note of where they came from. No licence text, no
  attribution line and no upstream URL travelled with them, and the
  project cannot reconstruct any of that now. Rather than leave the
  gap silent, it is recorded here.

  What follows from that:

    - Roxa claims no copyright and no licence over these files.
      They are NOT covered by the GPL-3.0 that covers Roxa's code,
      and nothing in this file should be read as relicensing them.
    - Roxa does not claim that they are in the public domain, nor
      that they may be freely redistributed. The absence of a
      recorded licence is not a licence.
    - They are rendered as they are: no file is edited, recoloured
      or otherwise modified.
    - The two-letter codes themselves are ISO 3166-1 alpha-2
      values, which are not subject to copyright. Only the
      artwork is in question.

  If you hold rights in any of these images and want them removed
  or replaced, contact roxacore.admin@gmail.com and the file will
  be taken down or swapped for an icon set whose licence is
  recorded here in full. Until such a set is chosen, treat these
  files as unlicensed material of unknown provenance.
